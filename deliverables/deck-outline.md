## 1. WildGround

A browser arcade with optional
Solana holder cosmetics.

## 2. The player problem

How can a casual game offer a visible token-holder identity without making every player connect a wallet?

A player should try the game first.
A holder should be able to choose a distinct appearance.

## 3. Alternative access designs

Immediate access to the game.
No holding-based cosmetic choice.

One wallet rule governs all access.
Players must connect before trying a match.

WildGround separates ordinary PLAY from optional SKINS.

## 4. The WildGround approach

PLAY opens a local match
against 1–6 bots.

Animal heads, matching trails
and emoji-filled territory.

## 5. The playable product

A match starts in the browser.
No download or wallet is required for PLAY.

Close a trail to claim territory.
Cut an exposed enemy trail.
Survive to win.

## 6. Use cases

Casual solo matches against bots.
Optional animal cosmetics for WG holders.
WATCH AI for spectator play.

Player onboarding pilot.
Repeat-session and cosmetic-use tests.
Multiplayer exploration after validation.

## 7. What is built

## 8. Why this is buildable now

The game and WG token are already live.

iDosGames handles signed wallet login.
The game reads the verified address.

The client derives the standard WG account.
PublicNode returns its current token balance.
