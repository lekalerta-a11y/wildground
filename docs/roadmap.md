# Roadmap

Proposed phases, not committed delivery dates or funded milestones.

## Published MVP

Local territory capture, 1–6 bots, spectator mode, animal skins and optional WG holding verification are implemented and published.

## Phase 1: verification and pilot

Validate a real holder's unlock, account switching and insufficient balance. Test desktop and touch onboarding.

Proposed pilot: recruit 20 players. Continue if at least 10 finish a match and at least 6 voluntarily start a second. If either threshold fails, improve controls and onboarding before expanding scope. Separately record wallet completion and cosmetic use. These are proposed success criteria, not results.

## Phase 2: reliability and retention

Improve RPC failure handling. Explore server verification or an indexer for extra token accounts only if player needs justify it. Test repeat sessions and bot tuning.

## Phase 3: multiplayer exploration

Evaluate authoritative servers and matchmaking after pilot evidence. Multiplayer, reward leaderboards, tournaments and on-chain inventory are not implemented.

## Business model hypothesis

The MVP does not charge players and has no demonstrated revenue model. Cosmetic purchases or platform partnerships are possible later experiments. Holding WG is an access rule, not project revenue or a return promise.
