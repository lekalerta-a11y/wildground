# Demo guide

## Free play

1. Open https://idosgames.com/app/XCQ1T7X1/.
2. Select PLAY, choose 1–6 opponents and START.
3. Steer with WASD, arrows, mouse or touch. Close a loop by returning to your territory.
4. Show territory share and minimap. Escape pauses.

No wallet is required.

## WG cosmetics

1. Return to the menu and open SKINS.
2. CONNECT & CHECK. The human wallet owner approves connection and the login message.
3. Confirm that the displayed address matches the connected wallet.
4. At least 100,000 WG in the standard ATA enables animal selection.
5. Show emoji head, matching trail and emoji-filled territory.
6. Below the threshold, demonstrate the locked state and return to PLAY.

Never fabricate a balance for a public demo. Use a real holder for positive-flow verification.
